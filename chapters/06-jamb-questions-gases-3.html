1. In the Haber process for the manufacture of ammonia, finely divided iron is used as:

Correct Answer: C — Finely divided iron serves as the catalyst to speed up the rate of reaction [32].

2. Nitrogen can best be obtained from a mixture of oxygen and nitrogen by passing the mixture over:

Correct Answer: D — Heated phosphorus reacts with and removes the oxygen, leaving nitrogen gas behind [32].

3. Which of the following is used in the manufacture of bleaching powder?

Correct Answer: B — Chlorine gas passed over slaked lime produces bleaching powder [32].

4. When steam is passed through red hot carbon, which of the following are produced?

Correct Answer: C — Passing steam over red hot carbon produces water gas, which is a mixture of Hydrogen ($H_2$) and Carbon(II) oxide ($CO$) [32].

5. When chlorine is passed into water and subsequently exposed to sunlight, the gas evolved is:

Correct Answer: C — Sunlight decomposes oxochlorate(I) acid ($HOCl$) to liberate oxygen gas ($O_2$) [32].

6. Magnesium ribbon was allowed to burn inside a given gas P leaving a white solid residue Q. Addition of water to Q liberated a gas which produced dense white fumes with a drop of hydrochloric acid. The gas P was:

Correct Answer: A — Magnesium burns in Nitrogen to form Magnesium nitride ($Mg_3N_2$). Adding water releases Ammonia gas ($NH_3$), which forms white fumes with $HCl$ [32].
