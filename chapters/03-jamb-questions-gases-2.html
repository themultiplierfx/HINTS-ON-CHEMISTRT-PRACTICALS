12. Sulphur (IV) oxide gas can act as:

Correct Answer: C — Sulphur (IV) oxide is widely used as a bleaching agent in the presence of moisture.

13. Which of the following is used as a rocket fuel?

Correct Answer: A — Concentrated \(HNO_3\) serves as a potent oxidizer in rocket fuel systems.

14. The bleaching action of chlorine is effective due to the presence of:

Correct Answer: B — Moisture/Water is essential for chlorine to generate its active oxochlorate(I) acid bleaching agent.

15. Sulphur (IV) oxide is a strong reducing agent in the presence of water due to the formation of:

Correct Answer: D — \(SO_2\) dissolves in water to release active reducing ions from trioxosulphate(IV) acid fractions.

16. The gas that gives brown colouration in the brown ring test is:

Correct Answer: B — Nitrogen (II) oxide (\(NO\)) reacts with iron(II) sulfate complex frames to form the diagnostic brown ring tracking nitrate verification layers.

17. Sulphur (IV) oxide bleaches by:

Correct Answer: B — Unlike chlorine, \(SO_2\) bleaches organic dye frameworks strictly via reduction.

18. Chlorine gas turns a damp starch-iodine paper:

Correct Answer: D — Chlorine displaces underlying iodine particles from the test paper substrate matrix, triggering the classic dark blue starch complex.
