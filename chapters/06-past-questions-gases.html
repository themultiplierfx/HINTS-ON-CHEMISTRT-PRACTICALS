Chapter 6

JAMB & SSCE Questions on Gases

Test your knowledge with practice questions drawn from past evaluation modules. Tap any option to review or click the validation button below each question card to check solutions.

QUESTION 1: A gas that turned a filter paper previously soaked in lead(II) ethanoate solution black is likely to be:

Correct Answer: B — Hydrogen sulphide ($H_2S$) reacts to form insoluble black lead sulphide ($PbS$) precipitates.

QUESTION 2: When chlorine is passed into water and subsequently exposed to bright sunlight, the dynamic gaseous product evolved is:

Correct Answer: C — Sunlight decomposes intermediate oxochlorate(I) acid ($HOCl$), releasing pure oxygen gas ($O_2$).

QUESTION 3: Clear solution of Ca(OH)₂ (lime water) forms a white precipitate when carbon(IV) oxide is bubbled into it for a short window due to the generation of:

Correct Answer: B — Insoluble calcium trioxocarbonate(IV) ($CaCO_3$) creates the distinct milkiness.
