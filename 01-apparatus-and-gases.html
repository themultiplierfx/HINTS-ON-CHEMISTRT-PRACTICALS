Let's Start With The Apparatus

To carry out tests on salts, the most important apparatus you will need is the test tubes. The boiling test tubes are for tests that require boiling. The ignition test tubes are for tests that require heating.

💡 20-Year Classroom Rule: Always use a test tube clamp to hold test tubes while boiling or heating its content. And always point the test tube in a slant position away from yourself and your colleagues to avoid dangerous spurting!

Introduction to Gases

The gas evolved helps us to identify the anion present in a given substance.

📢 Classroom Explanation: If you cannot identify CO₂ gas, how would you know the anion present is CO₃²⁻? Only a monkey can give birth to a monkey! Hahaha!! This is why you should know the gases at this point.
Gas Physical Characteristics Confirmatory Test Signature
Ammonia (NH₃) Pungent choking smell like "fermented urine". Turns litmus red to blue. Gives dense white fumes with concentrated HCl.
Hydrogen (H₂) Colourless and odourless. Neutral to litmus. Gives a “pop sound” when tested with a lighted splint.
Carbon(IV) Oxide (CO₂) Colourless and odourless. Slightly acidic. Turns lime water milky.
