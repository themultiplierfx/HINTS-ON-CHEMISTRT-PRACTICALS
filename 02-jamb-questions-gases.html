JAMB Questions on Gases 1

(Select an option or click the validation button below each card to reveal keys instantly.)

1. In the course of an experiment, two gases X and Y were produced. X turned wet lead ethanoate to black and Y bleached moist litmus paper. What are the element(s) in each of the gases X and Y respectively?

Correct Answer: A — Gas X is H₂S (Hydrogen and Sulphur) which blackens lead ethanoate, and Gas Y is Cl₂ (Chlorine) which bleaches litmus.

2. Which of the following gases will rekindle a brightly glowing splint?

Correct Answer: C — N₂O (Nitrogen(I) Oxide) characteristically rekindles a brightly glowing splint.

3. A gas that turned a filter paper previously soaked in lead ethanoate solution black is:

Correct Answer: B — Hydrogen sulphide gas turns lead ethanoate paper black.

4. A gas that can behave as reducing agent towards chlorine and as an oxidizing agent toward hydrogen sulphide is:

Correct Answer: C — SO₂ behaves as a reducing agent toward chlorine and an oxidizing agent toward H₂S.

5. Chlorine, bromine and iodine resemble one another in that they:

Correct Answer: A — Halogens dissolve in alkalis to create corresponding halate salts.

6. In which order are the following salts sensitive to light?

Correct Answer: D — Standard sensitivity trends follow the order AgCl > AgBr > AgI.
