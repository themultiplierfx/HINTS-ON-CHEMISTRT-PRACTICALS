JAMB Questions on Gases 4

(Select an option or click the validation button below each card to reveal keys instantly.)

1. In the Haber process for the manufacture of ammonia, the catalyst commonly used is finely divided:

Correct Answer: C — Finely divided iron is the standard catalyst used in the Haber process.

2. A quantity of air was passed through a weighed amount of alkaline pyrogallol. An increase in the weight of the pyrogallol would result from the absorption of:

Correct Answer: D — Alkaline pyrogallol is a specific absorber for oxygen gas.

3. Which of the following, in clear solution, forms a white precipitate when carbon(IV) oxide is bubbled into it for a short time?

Correct Answer: C — Ca(OH)₂ (lime water) turns milky due to insoluble calcium carbonate formation.

4. Which of the following gases dissolves in water vapour to produce acid rain during rainfall?

Correct Answer: D — Sulphur(IV) oxide dissolves to form acid rain components.

5. The gas formed when ammonium trioxonitrate (V) is heated with sodium hydroxide is:

Correct Answer: D — Heating any ammonium salt with an alkali like NaOH liberates ammonia gas.

6. Argon is used in gas-filled electric lamps because it helps to:

Correct Answer: B — Argon provides an unreactive environment that shields the filament from burning up or oxidizing.

7. Water can be identified by the use of:

Correct Answer: A — Water characteristically changes anhydrous copper(II) tetraoxosulphate(VI) from white to blue.
